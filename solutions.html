Solution examples

How does a challenge
become a working solution?

Illustrative examples to help you picture what can be built and tailored to your business. The actual scope is defined after we understand your operations and goals.

Illustrative examples—not client case studies
01 AI / CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE
✦

Intelligent customer service assistant

A bilingual assistant that answers from company knowledge and connects with WhatsApp and CRM.

Challenge

Repeated questions, slow responses, and knowledge scattered across files and people.

Solution

RAG Chatbot + AI Agent + WhatsApp API + CRM Automation.

Expected outcome

Always-on service, consistent answers, and faster routing to the right team.

02 ERP / AUTOMATION
▦

Unified business operations

Connect sales, inventory, accounting, and approvals in one central system.

Challenge

Disconnected spreadsheets, duplicate entry, and no real-time operational view.

Solution

Odoo or custom ERP + n8n Workflows + management dashboard.

Expected outcome

Less manual work, consistent data, and faster decisions across departments.

03 COMMERCE / MARKETPLACE
◇

Multi-vendor commerce platform

A marketplace that manages vendors, orders, payments, and commissions in one place.

Challenge

Fragmented sales channels and difficult order tracking and vendor settlement.

Solution

Multi-Vendor Platform + Payments + Order Management + APIs.

Expected outcome

Centralized operations, smooth buying, and easier vendor and catalog growth.

04 INDUSTRY / IoT
◉

Connected factory & predictive maintenance

Real-time monitoring of machines, energy, and faults through sensors and control dashboards.

Challenge

Unexpected downtime, manual tracking, and unclear causes of production loss.

Solution

Sensors + Edge Computing + MES + Predictive Maintenance.

Expected outcome

Live visibility, earlier alerts, and better maintenance and production planning.

05 DATA / EXECUTIVE BI
⌁

Executive data command center

An executive view that combines KPIs from multiple systems with performance and forecasts.

Challenge

Late reports, conflicting numbers, and no single view of company performance.

Solution

Data Warehouse + ETL + BI Dashboards + Forecasting.

Expected outcome

Trusted KPIs, faster monitoring, and earlier detection of opportunities and risks.

06 CLOUD / SECURITY
◎

Secure, scalable cloud foundation

Move systems to a resilient environment with automated releases, monitoring, backup, and protection.

Challenge

Recurring outages, manual deployment, and costs or risks that are hard to control.

Solution

Cloud Migration + Docker + CI/CD + Monitoring + DR.

Expected outcome

Higher reliability, faster releases, and better control of security and resources.
