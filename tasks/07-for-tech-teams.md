2. Group the capability areas into ~9 primary capability sections + a compact "supporting" block.

   - **Cloud & DevOps** — AWS/Azure/GCP, Docker/Kubernetes, CI/CD, hosting, migration, server mgmt.
   - **Cybersecurity** — pen testing, vuln assessment, SOC, network/endpoint, compliance, backup/DR.
   - **IoT & smart systems** — industrial automation/MES, predictive maintenance, smart buildings,
     sensors/firmware/edge.

     localization, loyalty, legal/compliance tech, no-code/low-code, accessibility (A11y).
