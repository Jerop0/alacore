Before we begin

Clear answers before you decide.

If your question is different, our assistant knows the details of our services—or you can speak directly with the team.

How do we start a project with ALA Core?

We begin with a short conversation to understand the goal, challenge, and current systems. Then we define an initial scope, deliverables, phases, and suitable timeline before implementation begins.

Should we use a custom system or a platform like Odoo?

It depends on your processes, budget, integrations, and growth plans. We assess both objectively: use an established platform when it fits, and build custom when your requirements create an advantage or are not covered well.

Can we start small and expand later?

Yes. We often recommend an MVP or first phase focused on the highest value and lowest risk, then add capabilities and integrations based on usage and results.

What happens after the system launches?

We can provide monitoring, support, maintenance, continuous improvement, and training. The support model and service level depend on system criticality, user count, and required coverage hours.

How do you handle security and company data?

We apply security by design, defining access, encryption, backup, and monitoring around the solution. Regulatory requirements are reviewed for your country and industry before implementation.
